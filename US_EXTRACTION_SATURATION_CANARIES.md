# U.S. Extraction-Saturation Canaries — First Research List

## What this is

Not GDP. Not GNP. Not a generic unemployment dashboard.

This is a short list of things to watch when the system may be running out of room to keep extracting from households — people cannot keep payments current, they cut ordinary spending, businesses lose customers, and weaker debt starts to break.

It cannot honestly predict a specific revolt or collapse date. It can show **broad household strain transmitting into demand failure and credit failure.**

## The nine canaries

| Bucket | Canary | Why it matters | Timing | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Household breaking point | New credit-card balances becoming 30+ days late | One of the earliest signs people are short of cash before formal default | Early | [NY Fed Household Debt and Credit](https://www.newyorkfed.org/microeconomics/hhdc) — Consumer Credit Panel/Equifax workbook |
| Household breaking point | Auto-loan balances 90+ days late | Severe payment failure; the honest national substitute for a supposedly universal repo statistic | Early-lagging | NY Fed Household Debt and Credit |
| Household breaking point | New foreclosures | Direct sign households have moved from strain into housing loss | Late confirmation | NY Fed Household Debt and Credit |
| Household breaking point | Household debt-service ratio | Required debt payments as a share of disposable income — how much room is left to absorb another bill | Current strain | [Federal Reserve / FRED `TDSP`](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/TDSP) |
| Corporate / credit machine | CCC-and-lower corporate-bond spread | The weakest corporate borrowers are getting expensive to refinance; credit markets are pricing future breakage | Early | [FRED `BAMLH0A3HYC`](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A3HYC) |
| Corporate / credit machine | C&I loan demand and lending standards | Shows firms pulling back from borrowing for inventory, payroll, expansion, and working capital — or banks closing the spigot | Early | [Fed SLOOS / FRED `DRTSCILM`](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DRTSCILM) plus lending-standards series |
| Corporate / credit machine | Business-loan delinquencies / charge-offs | Confirms business cash-flow pressure has become actual missed payments and losses | Confirmation | [FRED `DRBLACBS`](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DRBLACBS) / [business-loan charge-offs](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CORBLACBS) |
| Real demand failure | Restaurant and bar sales, adjusted for food-away-from-home inflation | Fast read on whether households are cutting small discretionary pleasures and ordinary social spending | Fast, but noisy | [Census/FRED `RSFSDP`](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RSFSDP). Must be inflation-adjusted: nominal sales alone can rise while actual visits fall. |
| Real demand failure | Commercial / Chapter 11 bankruptcies and establishment deaths | Confirms lost demand is destroying actual businesses, not merely hurting sentiment | Late confirmation | [American Bankruptcy Institute statistics](https://www.abi.org/newsroom/bankruptcy-statistics) and [BLS Business Employment Dynamics](https://www.bls.gov/bdm/) |

## The signal we actually care about

A single bad line means little. The real warning is when all three buckets deteriorate together:

1. **People miss payments** — cards/autos/foreclosures rise.
2. **Credit turns against weak firms** — CCC spreads widen and lending retreats.
3. **Ordinary demand falls** — restaurants, small businesses, and then firms fail.

That is the shape of the parasite running out of blood: households cannot borrow or cut any further; then the businesses that depend on their spending lose the customers; then credit losses expose the same strain inside the financial system.

## Simple dashboard rule

- **Watch:** one worsening household canary plus one worsening credit/demand canary for two reporting releases.
- **Broad strain:** at least one worsening indicator in all three buckets.
- **Do not call it a collapse forecast:** describe it as *household strain transmitting into demand and credit failure.*

## Important source honesty

- There is no clean, transparent, free nationwide U.S. repo series good enough to make a headline metric. Auto 30+/90+ delinquency is the defensible precursor.
- Foreclosures and bankruptcies are late; use them as confirmation, not as early warnings.
- Restaurant sales are nominal. They must be deflated and ideally paired with visits/closures before claiming customers are disappearing.
- Bond spreads are markets pricing risk, not proof that households are already failing. They become meaningful when household and demand signals agree.
